# Website review for Lefkoşa Türk Belediyesi

Tuğberk Ata · 9 November 2026

## What is working well
- **Page addresses are clear and easy to share.** Addresses such as lefkosabelediyesi.org/sehrin-tarihi are plain Turkish words, so residents can read them, guess them and send them on WhatsApp without them breaking.
- **Broken links are handled kindly.** If someone follows an old or mistyped link, they see a friendly apology in Turkish and two buttons to carry on, and Google is correctly told that the page does not exist.

## The three things to fix first

### 1. Make the contact form usable for everyone
- **What visitors experience:** People who use a screen reader (a program that reads the page aloud for blind users) cannot tell which box is for their name and which for their phone number. People who use only a keyboard cannot see where they are on the page. When a box is left empty, it only turns red, with no words explaining what is wrong.
- **Why it matters to you:** The "Lefkoşalılar Söylüyor" form is how residents send you their opinions and complaints. Some residents, often older or disabled people, cannot use it at all. The accessibility button on your site also promises a standard that the form does not yet meet.
- **What to ask your web developer:** "Please connect each form label to its box, add a short written error message under any box that is wrong, and show a clear frame around whatever is selected with the keyboard."
- **How to check it is fixed:** Press the Tab key on the home page. You should see a clear frame move from link to link. On the form, click the word "Adınız": the cursor should jump into the box.

### 2. Make the home page load faster on phones
- **What visitors experience:** On a slow mobile connection the home page stays completely white for several seconds, then shows empty squares where icons should be. The large moving background video is most of what the page downloads.
- **Why it matters to you:** Many residents visit on their phones. A blank page looks broken, and the video uses up their mobile data for decoration only.
- **What to ask your web developer:** "Please replace the background video with a compressed photo, show the loading animation that is already in the site's code, and let browsers keep images for longer."
- **How to check it is fixed:** Open the home page on your phone using mobile data, not Wi-Fi. Something should appear within a second or two, never a blank white screen.

### 3. Help Google and screen readers understand the site
- **What visitors experience:** The home page has no main title marked for computers to recognise, the site opens on two different addresses (with and without "www"), and the nineteen partner logos are all read aloud simply as "Logo".
- **Why it matters to you:** Your pages may appear lower in Google results, and blind residents cannot tell the orchestra from the animal shelter.
- **What to ask your web developer:** "Please mark 'Nasıl yardımcı olabiliriz?' as the page's main title, send the 'www' address automatically to the main address, and give every logo its project name."
- **How to check it is fixed:** Type www.lefkosabelediyesi.org into your browser. It should switch by itself to lefkosabelediyesi.org.

Thank you for providing so many services online for the people of Lefkoşa; these small changes would make them easier to use for every resident.
